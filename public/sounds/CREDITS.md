# Weather sounds

Two short loops, trimmed from recordings and levelled. The Lounge plays them from this folder; nothing is downloaded while you use Codeshi.

| File | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rain.m4a` | "Rain Sound" by boons_freak, https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/nature-rain-sound-188158/ (80 seconds) | Pixabay Content License |
| `snow.m4a` | "Sound of Falling Snow" by mind_out_of_office, Pixabay sound 211055 (7.5 seconds, raised in level) | Pixabay Content License |

The loops are cut so the end blends into the start.
